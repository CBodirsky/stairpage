Just a quick ARG project thrown together for fun. Short lived and far from as detailed as I wanted to ultimately make it. But a fun way of demonstrating the lighter side of my skills in HTML/Javascript, 
Photoshop, and creative writing and design. Don't mind the lack of organization.
Week 1:
index is the original page (linked from the QR code on StairsWarning.pdf posted on student notice boards on campus). 
Week 2: 
index received an update with more content and stairsDoc.jpg which was also posted on boards (and now quickly taken down)
Week 3:
oac is the homepage for a defunct OAC site. Images are purposely broken, content is redacted, and generally designed to raise questions of any actual presence and purpose. DMStudentLedgerApril2023.doc 
was posted on boards (and also quickly taken down).

At this point I decided the project was getting little attention and quickly getting shut down presumably by staff checking the boards. So I called it quits. I did have plans to add a final official notice from OAC. 
The anomaly had been resolved and while there were some losses of assets (i.e. students and agents), these are within acceptable parameters. More or less my own take of a SPC Foundation universe getting wrapped up for finals week.
